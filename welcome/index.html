Eye Dropper Color Picker is installed

Pick any color on your screen in one click. Three quick steps to get started.

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon ➊ in the Chrome toolbar, then the pin ➋ next to Eye Dropper Color Picker. The icon stays visible next to the address bar.

Pick a color

Click the icon ➌ (or press Alt+C), then press Enter or click Pick color. A magnifier appears — click any pixel: on a web page, in a video, or even outside the browser window.

Copy in your format

The code is copied automatically. Switch between HEX, RGB, HSL, HSB, Tailwind and a CSS variable — one click copies the selected format. Colors you pick are saved under Recent; pin your favorites to the Palette and export it when you need it.

Tips

  • Alt+C opens the picker; Enter or Space starts picking; Esc cancels. You can change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Need several colors in a row? After each pick the button keeps focus — just press Enter again.
  • Picking from a video? Pause it on the right frame first.
  • Want to pick a color from an image file? Use the online image color picker.
