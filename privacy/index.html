Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Summary

The Eye Dropper Color Picker extension does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. It has no analytics, no accounts and no servers. It requests three permissions: storage (your color history, palette and settings in your own Chrome profile), and activeTab + scripting, used only by the Precision picker to take a one-time snapshot of the tab you are looking at, on your click. Nothing ever leaves your device.

The extension

  • Pick color: uses the browser's built-in EyeDropper API to read the color of the pixel you click. It does not capture screenshots and does not read page content.
  • Precision picker: when you click Precision (or press Shift+Space), the extension takes a single snapshot of the visible part of the current tab, shows it to you with a magnifier so you can choose a pixel, and discards it as soon as you pick a color or press Esc. The snapshot stays in the memory of that tab only: it is never saved, never sent anywhere and never read by us. Access to the tab is granted by Chrome only for the tab you clicked in and only for that moment; the extension has no access to the websites you visit at any other time.
  • What it stores: the colors you picked (up to 24 recent and 48 pinned), your preferred format, theme and settings. This data lives in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may synchronize between your own devices if you are signed into Chrome. We cannot access it.
  • What it sends: nothing. The extension makes no network requests. The only two pages it opens are this website's Welcome page (once, after installation) and the Uninstall page (when you remove it), as standard browser tabs.
  • Permissions: storage, activeTab and scripting (the last two only for the Precision picker, as described above). No host permissions, no tabs, no clipboard-read permission. Copying to the clipboard happens only when you click or press a key.

This website

  • Online image color picker: images you drop or paste are processed entirely in your browser with the Canvas API and are never uploaded.
  • Analytics: we may use a privacy-friendly, cookieless page-view counter to understand how many people visit the site and the Welcome page. It does not identify you and does not track you across sites.
  • Uninstall feedback form: optional and anonymous. If you submit it, your selected reason and comment are stored in a form backend (Google Forms) so we can read them. Do not include personal information in the comment.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The extension and website are not directed to children under 13 and collect no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Material changes to what the extension does with data would also be described in the extension's changelog.
